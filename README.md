# Bright-Coffee-Shop-Case-Study
---
## Overview
The New CEO of Bright Coffee Shop want to know the performance of the business. This repo contains detailed business analysis for the coffee shop with 3 stores over period of January 2023 to June 2023. The key pointsis to understand revenue trends, customer behavious, sales strong peak, sales weak peak, best performing products and products that need more attention, and identify strategic models to upsell sales
## Objective
1.Business overall performance.
2.Monthly performance.
3.Weekly performance of sales.
4.Product category performance.
5.Store comparison on performance.
6.Revenue generated in 3-hour interval.
7.Comparing product category amongst stores sales.

## Recommendation
Recommendations to the CEO
1. Increase customer spending: Introduce coffee-and-bakery combos and upselling to increase the average transaction value.
2. Priorities best-selling products: Maintain sufficient stock of coffee, tea and bakery products, which generate the majority of revenue.
3. Optimize staffing: Allocate more employees during peak hours, particularly between 9 AM and 12 PM.
4. Improve store performance: Develop store-specific promotions based on customer product preferences.(Develop Marketing strategy before cold seasons)
5. Reduce operating costs: Review evening sales against staffing and operating expenses.
6. Monitor performance: Track revenue, gross profit, customer spending and product sales regularly.

Overall recommendation: Focus on increasing revenue per customer, improving operational efficiency and strengthening profitability rather than relying solely on attracting more customers.
